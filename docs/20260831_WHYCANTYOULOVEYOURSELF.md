You cannot understand it without self-connection, and you cannot connect without accepting/loving yourself. It is one big circle.  

If you are starting or struggling, drop everything else and go back to the basics. Look at yourself: How do I feel about myself? About how others see me? Does it bother me what they think? You cannot be of service to others if you cannot even be with yourself, connect with yourself, love yourself. Stop looking elsewhere. Stop feeling like you need to “do” something. The only thing you need to do is take care of you by becoming the highest version of yourself. If that simply means learning to love yourself and taking life in stride without taking everything so seriously — that is it.
