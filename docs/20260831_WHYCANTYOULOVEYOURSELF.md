Yes, Mel is of service — but she was not seeking it when she began, and neither should you. Any greater-scale involvement does not come from seeking. It comes from aligning with your highest path, which then opens what you are meant to do. You cannot seek your purpose; **you become it**.  

Purpose is already aligned with your journey, your experiences, what you have understood or overcome. You do not have to do it on a bigger scale. Simply by living and becoming you — living authentically — you **are** living your purpose and being of service. Anything beyond that will then be presented to you; it will become clear.  

Do not live thinking you have to be of service in order to ascend, become something, or prove your worth. That is no different from religion: trying to be good/kind so that God/Source looks favourably upon you. That is not what this existence is about.  

This ties directly into the previous day’s message on distorted intuition. Your intuition becomes distorted when you are not connected to the self. Why can’t many people connect? The biggest reason is lack of self-love. They look outward to fix and heal without ever going back to the self.  

Connection always starts with the self and then moves outward. If you struggle with others while ignoring how you feel about yourself, you will not make progress. At the most basic human level this begins with accepting how you look, your body, your actions, the past and the present. You do not have to “love” the appearance, but if you do not accept it (and feel less-than because society deems it so), how can you connect with the part of you that includes all of that? What you connect with accepts all of it. If the human cannot, there is misalignment.  

Without that connection you cannot know your purpose. We are not here to serve others, yet we all have a purpose of some sort — often revealed in the smallest, most benign, hidden ways. You cannot understand it without self-connection, and you cannot connect without accepting/loving yourself. It is one big circle.  

If you are starting or struggling, drop everything else and go back to the basics. Look at yourself: How do I feel about myself? About how others see me? Does it bother me what they think? Do I feel less-than? Do I need to live up to others’ expectations? Am I afraid of being judged? If the answers are non-favourable, start there. Everything builds from there.  

You cannot be of service to others if you cannot even be with yourself, connect with yourself, love yourself. Stop looking elsewhere. Stop feeling like you need to “do” something. The only thing you need to do is take care of you by becoming the highest version of yourself. If that simply means learning to love yourself and taking life in stride without taking everything so seriously — that is it.
